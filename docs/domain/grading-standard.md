# TrueRefurb OS — Grading & Quality Assurance Standard

TrueRefurb OS grading follows CTA-2053 and R2v3 standards to categorize refurbished electronics into standardized cosmetic and functional quality tiers.

## Cosmetic Quality Matrix

| Grade | Label | Screen / Glass Integrity | Housing / Bezel | Battery Retention | Resale Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A+** | Pristine / Like New | Flawless. Zero micro-abrasions under 500 lux light | No rubs, scuffs, or corner dings | &ge; 90% OEM capacity | 1.15x Baseline |
| **A** | Excellent | Minor micro-scratches invisible at 30cm with screen on | Negligible hairline rub marks | &ge; 85% OEM capacity | 1.00x Baseline |
| **B** | Good | Light scratches invisible during active display usage | Light edge scuffs or minor corner dings | &ge; 80% OEM capacity | 0.88x Baseline |
| **C** | Fair | Moderate visible scratches, no structural glass cracks | Noticeable casing scuffs or small chassis dents | &ge; 80% OEM capacity | 0.72x Baseline |
| **D** | Clearance / B2B Bulk | Heavy cosmetic wear, fully functioning core logic | Heavy abrasion or minor frame warp | Functioning | 0.55x Baseline |
| **Parts** | Harvest / Salvage | Cracked OLED, broken digitizer, or logic failure | Scrapped for OEM modules | Below threshold | 0.30x Baseline |

## Mandatory Functional Inspection Suite

Every unit entering TrueRefurb OS must clear 100% of the following automated and technician-led checks:
1. **Power & Thermal**: Charging port draw (PD wattage), battery cycle retention, thermal dissipation under load.
2. **Display & Touch**: Digitizer multi-touch matrix, dead pixel color gamut test, 120Hz ProMotion/refresh rate sync.
3. **Biometrics & Security**: Infrared FaceID / TouchID / In-display ultrasonic sensor, cryptographically verified data erasure.
4. **Wireless & Connectivity**: 5G Sub-6/mmWave RSSI, Wi-Fi 6E dual-band, Bluetooth 5.3 pairing, NFC Apple/Google Pay.
5. **Acoustic & Cameras**: Dual microphone frequency range, stereo speaker balance, LiDAR, optical zoom and image stabilization.
